## Limits
- Labels are relative to the lists and their procedure, not to clinical truth. Flip cases are not real patients: findings listed for the gold diagnosis only are removed and findings listed for the other only are added. Lines about a relative use the prefix 'About the patient's <relative>: '.
- DDXPlus patients are generated from the same knowledge base, so no unedited patient shows a finding outside the list of the own diagnosis; the support check measures the lists against their own generator, not against clinical practice.
- By its authors' description the knowledge base guided the construction of MedEinst's trap cases; handing the lists to a model changes what that benchmark measures (Appendix E).
