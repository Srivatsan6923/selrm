  does forbid calling the rule a guideline rule.

## 9. Semantics actually implemented

Measured on all test and development cases (47,600 cases) and the
library (791 criteria).

**Several mentions.**
- A finding criterion holds if any mention it counts has status present.
- A measurement criterion counts exactly one value, the patient's current value.
  `Criterion.evaluate` raises an error on zero or two counted values, so such states are never
  generated: 56,592 of
  56,592 measured inputs have exactly one.
- Earlier values are separate mentions with time past, and the criterion does not count them
  (6,868 inputs carry one).
- No measurement criterion counts past values
  (0 of 332).
- There is no rule that selects the most recent of several values.

**Conflicting values.** None is generated.
- A case holds at most one mention of each finding concept:
  0 of
  56,740 finding inputs have two or more.
- A measured input has one current value; an older value is dated or marked as replaced.
- A generic absence line that could deny another present concept is left out.

**Units.**
- One unit per measured input across all templates: 19
  of 21 inputs have one, and
  arterial_ph, inr have none.
- The unit is stated in the rule text for 287
  of the 287 criteria with a unit.
- No edit changes a unit, and no rule needs a conversion.
- Units: age: years; alt_enzyme: U/L; arterial_ph: none; bmi: kg/m2; bun: mg/dL; calf_swelling: cm; creatinine: mg/dL; egfr: mL/min/1.73; heart_rate: /min; hemoglobin: g/dL; inr: none; map: mmHg; neutrophils: x10^9/L; platelets: x10^9/L; potassium: mmol/L; rr: /min; sbp: mmHg; spo2: %; temperature: C; wbc: x10^9/L; weight: kg.

**Threshold inclusivity.**
- Operators: <: 61; >: 151; >=: 85; <=: 35. They are stated as
  "above"/"below" (strict) and "or more"/"or less" (inclusive) and checked by
  `tests/test_library.py`.
- Inclusivity belongs to the criterion, not to the input: the same measured input appears with
  strict and inclusive operators in different rules.
- A value exactly at a strict threshold is the boundary near-miss.
- At an inclusive threshold the same value is a flip:
  411 of the
  1,198 numeric flips at inclusive thresholds lie
  exactly on the stated threshold
  (402 of
  1,109 outside L3-alt, whose texts
  state the altered threshold).

**Reference date.** None.
- No case states the date of the visit
  (0 cases do).
- A mention is current or past. 8,117 of
  13,591 past mentions carry a year, from
  2,005 to 2,024.
  That is at least two years before the generator's internal year
  2,026, which is not shown.
- The other past mentions say "years ago", "previously", "last month", or (superseded tier)
  "measured yesterday ... replaced", or describe a relative's past.

**Window boundaries.** None in the programs.
- No criterion's program has a time window.
- The only rule text that names one is s3_jhfrat: "one fall
  in the six months before this admission".
- Its program counts current falls only, and its past falls are dated two or more years back. Its
  label therefore relies on the unstated year.
- This affects 23 test and development
  groups. In 3 of them
  the near-miss is a past fall.

**Omitted information.**
- An unmentioned history item is absent; an unmentioned measurement is unknown (`engine.ledger`).
- Labelled cases always state every measured input.
- Missing twins replace a finding line with an "unknown" line, or drop a measurement line.
- In finding bases the target concept is never named
  (0 of
  5,156).
  - 3,572 carry a related general line
    consistent with absence that does not name it.
    1,953 of these contain a
    negation of a broader class (e.g. "Drug allergies: none known."). The rest are positive
    statements (e.g. "Walks steadily with good balance.").
  - 1,584 say nothing.
  - So no base states the required absence explicitly.
- Context findings (criteria other than the target) are unmentioned in
  7,048 of
  36,116 context finding inputs.

**Near-miss attributes.**
- A subject near-miss differs from its flip only in the person.
- A negation near-miss is the patient's current denial, so on criteria that count past or family
  mentions it can differ from the flip in time or subject as well as status:
  670 of
  2,180 negation near-misses do.

What each session checked:

   literal reading of the rule and the case text?
2. **Consistency:** lines against each other, against the setting and header, and plausibility.
3. **Rendering:** grammar, units, decimals, broken text.
4. **Give-aways:** any line or wording that reveals the answer.
5. **Missing twins:** is the decisive input really unknown?

The instructions, the full reports and the rendered samples are in `audit/model_review/`.

What they found:
- **Round 1.** The sessions flagged groups whose stated answer a literal reader could dispute:
  11, 9, 10, 12 per session.
  - Most were missing twins whose "not recorded / not documented" wording reads as "absent".

  - No stored label disagreed with the program.
  - Fixes before the freeze:
    - "unknown" wording for missing inputs;
    - condition labels that name the counted subjects;
    - no "on arrival" in current-value templates;
    - presentation edits that keep every fact;
    - absence lines that never name the concept by a synonym;
    - setting exclusions;
    - subject near-misses at the flip's time, so that they differ from the flip in the person
      only. Negation near-misses were not aligned; see section 9.
- **Round 2.** Disputed answers: 0, 0 per session.
  - Consistency and rendering findings were fixed before the freeze: drawn values clashing with the
    setting, alternatives contraindicated by the condition, and the diastolic rendering.

## 11. Known issues

0. The following are semantic limits, not violations, and the paper
