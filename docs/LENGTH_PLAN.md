## 1. Current length

ARR long papers allow 8 pages of main text; Limitations, Ethical
Considerations, references and appendices do not count. The Conclusion must
therefore end on page 8, every table and figure of the main text must sit on
pages 1-8, and the Limitations section may start at the top of page 9 at the
latest.

**How it is measured.** With PyMuPDF, the main text ends where the
`Limitations` heading starts. A column holds 52 body lines at a pitch of
13.55 pt (first line at y=73.5 pt, last at y=764.5 pt on a full column), so a
page is 104 lines. Length = full pages before the Limitations page, plus 0.5
if the heading is in the right column, plus 0.5 x the share of that column
above the heading. Every float defined before `\section*{Limitations}` is also
located in the PDF, and the main text ends at the later of the heading and the
bottom of the last such float. In both v13 builds all main-text floats are
placed before the heading.

**Calibration (tectonic against pdflatex).** Both builds put every float on
the same page at the same height; the difference is line breaking (XeTeX has
no microtype font expansion) and it accumulates through the text. Section
starts, in pages, pdflatex / tectonic: Related Work 1.499 / 1.517; Section 3
2.722 / 2.760; Experimental Setup 5.850 / 5.935; Results 7.318 / 7.453;
Conclusion 10.769 / 10.935; Limitations 10.869 / 11.038. The whole-document
offset is 0.169 pages (17.6 lines). Matched block by block, 67 main-text
blocks occur in both builds; 14 of them differ (twelve by +1 line, one by +2,
one by -1), net +13 lines; the rest of the offset lies in blocks that could
not be matched (the two display equations, "External data and protocol") and
in spacing.

Rule used below: a tectonic length T corresponds to a pdflatex length between
T - 0.169 (whole-document offset) and T - (offset of the blocks still in the
main text). For the final trial that second offset is 0.067 pages (7 lines).
It leaves out four paragraphs without a measured counterpart: the three new or
rewritten ones (the pointers of moves 11 and 12, the shortened 7.5 paragraph)
and "External data and protocol", which is not matched in v13 either;
paragraphs shortened by a move are counted with their v13 offset. Predictions
are judged at the long end of the range.

## 3. Proposed moves, in priority order

Principles. Appendix-like detail first (proof, setup and protocol detail,
definitions already given in the appendix), then floats whose numbers the
prose reports, then secondary analyses, then related-work detail, and the
overview figure last. Nothing is deleted: every moved block keeps its labels,
so existing references now point into the appendix, and a pointer sentence is
left where the block was. Four sentences that repeat what an appendix
paragraph already says are dropped from the main text (marked "duplicate");
their content stays in that appendix paragraph. Moved floats change `[t]` to
`[!ht]` (starred floats keep `[t]`); a table that carries
`% <tables:name>` markers (P0.3) must move together with its markers.
Appendix letters A-H do not change; a new appendix I is added at the end.
Removing Section 4 renumbers Sections 5-8 as 4-7.

"Measured" is the change of the main-text length at that step of the trial
build (tectonic lines, Section 5). Steps are cumulative, so a step's figure
depends on the order (float placement, column breaks).

## 6. Residual risks

1. **Calibration.** No pdflatex is available here; the conversion rests on
   the two v13 builds. The final check must be made on the authors' pdflatex
   PDF: Limitations heading on page 9 at the latest, no main-text float after
   it.
2. **Placeholders filled.** Replacing the 145 `\ph{tbd}` of the main text (in
   the final trial) by four-character values lengthens it by 2.0 lines in
   tectonic, within the spare lines of moves 1-20. In the 979e42d version,
   whose generated tables print `tbd` in more cells, the same test replaces
   242 placeholders and adds 0.9 lines.
3. **Standard deviations in cells.** `make_tables.py` prints mean `\sd{s.d.}`
   once two or more seeds exist. With `\sd{0.0}` added to every seeded cell of
   Tables 3 and 4, Table 3 (factorial, single column) becomes 29.29 pt wider
   than the column (overfull) and needs a layout decision (`\resizebox`, which
   shrinks the font, or s.d. only in the appendix seeds table). Table 4 is
   already scaled to the text width, so its font shrinks further; the main
   text became 4.9 lines shorter in that simulation. A main-text seeds table
   would cost space; `tables/seeds.tex` belongs in the appendix.
4. **The authors' rewrite (H6).** The introduction, the discussion of
   results, the error analysis and the development history are to be
   rewritten. Every line added to the main text comes out of the 15.7 lines
   that remain at the long end of the range.
5. **Float placement.** Edits before a float can push it past the Conclusion
   (step 11). Table 4 is a full-width float that LaTeX can only place at the
   top of a page; if the text before it changes, it can move a page and leave
   or fill a gap. Check positions after every edit.
6. **Concurrent changes to `main.tex`.** P0.2/P0.3 are adding `\res` macros,
   table markers and generated bodies. Moves are specified by first and last
   words; where those words contain numbers or placeholders that `\res` may
   replace (moves 11, 14 and 17), cut by sentence. Table bodies must keep
   their markers. Documents that cite table or section numbers (FINAL_TASKS
   files, PAPER_CONTEXT, status board) will be out of step after
   renumbering; `make_tables.py` uses names.
7. **Draft-only text.** The main text still carries draft remarks (Table 3's
   caption "Values in black are measured (seed 0)", "At seed 0" in 7.2).
   Their final wording will change the length in either direction.
8. **Self-contained main text.** Reviewers are not required to read
   appendices. The pointers name what moved, but caveats now in the appendix
   (statistical protocol, what follows by construction, MedCEG) should be
   checked by the authors against what they want a reviewer to see first.
