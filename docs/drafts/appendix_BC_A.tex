\paragraph{Manifest and provenance.}
The released manifest gives, for each of the \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.library@int} rules:
\begin{itemize}
\item its source or specification;
\item the text shown in prompts;
\item the program and the applicability semantics of each criterion;
\item its tests and its version.
\end{itemize}
The \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.sampled_by_kind.constraint@int} grammar-sampled rules are synthetic.
For each of the \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.hand_written@int} hand-written rules, we looked for the source
document and recorded the supporting text, every simplification and every contradiction with the source.
The source record was verified online for \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.sources_verified_online@int} rules. By
kind:
\begin{itemize}
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.hand_written_provenance_by_kind.score.simplified_source@int} scoring rules are
simplified source rules;
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.hand_written_provenance_by_kind.constraint.simplified_source@int} constraint
rules are simplified source rules;
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.hand_written_provenance_by_kind.constraint.synthetic@int} constraint rules
are synthetic;
\item no rule restates a source verbatim.
\end{itemize}
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.sources_with_contradictions@int} rules contradict their source in at least one
respect, for example a threshold or an alternative the source does not support. Model agents did this
search, and a second agent tried to refute each record. No author has yet checked the formalisations
against their sources. Because every prompt states the rule, correctness is relative to the stated text
for all rules, and no rule is presented as clinical guidance.

\paragraph{Constraint rules and claims.}
Each constraint rule states a default option, one or more conditions and an alternative. The hand-written
rules were written directly as programs together with their rule text. Tests execute every threshold one
step below, at and above it, and check that the operator wording of the text matches the program. The
remaining constraint rules are sampled from a typed grammar. The sampler never pairs a condition with an
alternative that the condition rules out, or with a setting it contradicts. The two options of a rule are
plain orders whose lengths differ by at most
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/library_semantics.constraint_claim_word_diff_max@int} words.

\paragraph{Rendering audit.}
Before the freeze, \res{sum/A-AUDIT/reviewers.sessions@int} LLM agent sessions read
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/reviewers.groups@int} rendered groups in two rounds, and no person took part. Each session
checked labels under a literal reading of the rule and the case, consistency and plausibility, rendering,
and give-aways. No stored label disagreed with the program.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Round 1.} The sessions marked some answers as disputable under a literal reading. These were
mostly missing-input lines worded ``not recorded'', which reads as absent, plus a few condition labels and
time phrases. Their wording was changed.
\item \textbf{Round 2.} No disputed answers.
\item Presentation edits that changed a fact and alternatives ruled out by their condition were removed.
\item Subject near-misses were aligned with the time of their flip. Negation near-misses were not, so
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.negation_near_misses_differing_in_more_than_status@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.negation_near_misses@int} differ from the flip in time or
subject as well as status.
\end{itemize}
These sessions read pre-freeze versions of the generator. The authors' blind reading of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/sample_sheets.groups@int} frozen groups is the human check of text fidelity and labels.

\paragraph{Splits and overlap.}
In each of \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.folds@int} folds, 5 of the \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rules.signature_classes@int}
signature classes are held out for L2. Between the training rules of fold 1 and L2:
\begin{itemize}
\item canonical programs, parameter-normalised structures and rule texts are not shared
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.program_pct@1}\%, \res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.structure_pct@1}\% and
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.rule_text_pct@1}\%);
\item no rendering template is shared (\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.templates_shared@int} shared);
\item among L2 rules with a source, \res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.source_shared_with_train_pct@1}\% share a
source document with training;
\item criterion subexpressions are mostly shared (\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.subexpression_pct@1}\%), so L2
tests new compositions of familiar conditions.
\end{itemize}
Cue words for relatives, negation and time are split like the templates. The line a flip or near-miss adds
never contains a training cue word (\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.near_lines_with_train_cue_pct@1}\% of
near-miss lines). Elsewhere, \res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.cases_with_train_cue_pct@1}\% of L2 cases contain
one as an ordinary word. Patient states, taken without their rule, recur:
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.patient_states_shared_any_rule@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/overlap.L2.patient_states@int} L2 base states also occur in training.

\paragraph{L3-alt.}
Each of the \res{sum/A-AUDIT/l3alt.groups@int} groups moves exactly one threshold of a measured criterion.
The flip lies between the original and the altered threshold
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/l3alt.flips_between_thresholds_pct@1}\%), so the original rule gives the other answer
on it. Its near-miss kinds are therefore the numeric ones only: numeric
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/l3alt.near_miss_kinds.numeric@int}), at the threshold
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/l3alt.near_miss_kinds.boundary@int}) and an older value
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/l3alt.near_miss_kinds.time@int}).

\paragraph{Invariants.}
Every edit is checked on the target claim by executing the program. A flip must change the claim, and a
near-miss or a presentation edit must not; this holds for rules that combine criteria too. Flips are
decisive by construction: the other criteria are held met or unmet so that the target alone decides the
conclusion, so no proposal is ever rejected because a second criterion changes. The only rejected proposals
are presentation edits that would change nothing but the header
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/rejects.logged.L2.rejected@int} of \res{sum/A-AUDIT/rejects.logged.L2.proposed@int} in
L2). A missing twin is kept only if two completions of the decisive input, the base and the flip mention,
reach both outcomes, which is sufficient for ``undetermined''. The equalities $h_k(x)=0$,
$h_k(\xf)=h_k(\xn)=1$ ($h_k\equiv 1$ for measured inputs) are checked on the rendered text, and every ledger
quote must occur in its case. The program runs on the generator's state; nothing is parsed from the text.
We re-executed every registered test and development set and found
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/target_claim_check.violations@int} violations:
\begin{itemize}
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/target_claim_check.coverage.stored_labels@int} stored labels;
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/target_claim_check.coverage.read_labels@int} reading labels;
\item the triplet invariants of \res{sum/A-AUDIT/target_claim_check.coverage.triplet_groups@int} groups;
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/target_claim_check.coverage.missing_twins@int} missing twins.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Semantics.}
\emph{Several mentions.} A finding criterion holds if any mention it counts has status present. A
measurement criterion counts exactly one value, the patient's current value. Earlier values are reported as
past and are not counted. Every labelled case states exactly one current value of each measured input
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.numeric_inputs_one_counted_value@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.numeric_inputs@int}); missing twins omit the decisive one on
purpose. \emph{Conflicting values.} A case holds at most one mention of each finding, so a counted
``absent'' and a counted ``present'' never meet. \emph{Numbers.} Each measured input has one unit across
all templates, and the rule text states it
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/library_semantics.numeric_criteria_unit_in_rule_text@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/library_semantics.numeric_criteria_with_a_unit@int} criteria). No edit changes a unit.
Threshold inclusivity is fixed per criterion and stated in the rule text. A value exactly at a strict
threshold is its own near-miss kind. At an inclusive threshold the same value is a flip
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.numeric_flips_at_inclusive_threshold@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.numeric_flips_inclusive@int} such flips).
\emph{Time.} A mention is current or past, and a past mention may carry a year
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.past_mentions_with_year@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.past_mentions@int} do). Cases state no reference date, and no
program has a time window. One rule text names a window, which its program approximates by counting
current events only (\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.groups_rule_text_names_a_window@int}
groups, listed as a known issue). The rule-side items and the registered criteria state a visit date;
there, ``within $W$ months'' counts an event on or after the date exactly $W$ months before the visit,
and the rule text says so. \emph{Omission.} Measurements that are not given are unknown, and history
items that are not mentioned are absent, unless the case says they are unknown. This is a stipulation of
the generator. No finding base names the decisive concept.
\begin{itemize}
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.finding_bases_generic_absence_line@int} of
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.finding_bases@int} finding bases carry a general line
consistent with its absence. \res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.finding_bases_generic_line_with_negation@int}
of these negate a broader class.
\item \res{sum/A-AUDIT/semantics.all_test_and_dev.finding_bases_no_line@int} say nothing.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Near-miss kinds.}
In L2 each kind accounts for 20\% of the triplets: numeric (\res{sum/A-AUDIT/near_miss_kinds.L2.numeric.n@int}),
at a strict threshold (\res{sum/A-AUDIT/near_miss_kinds.L2.boundary.n@int}), another subject
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/near_miss_kinds.L2.subject.n@int}), explicit absence
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/near_miss_kinds.L2.negation.n@int}) and a time outside the predicate
(\res{sum/A-AUDIT/near_miss_kinds.L2.time.n@int}). For criteria whose predicate counts past or family
findings, such mentions are flips.

L2 triplets per requirement (a triplet can meet several):
\begin{itemize}
\item rule-dependent time scope: \res{sum/A-AUDIT/requirements.L2.rule_dependent_time_scope@int};
\item an older value beside the current one: \res{sum/A-AUDIT/requirements.L2.temporal_selection@int};
\item composition with another condition met:
\res{sum/A-AUDIT/requirements.L2.composition_with_other_condition_met@int};
\item numerical semantics at a threshold: \res{sum/A-AUDIT/requirements.L2.numerical_semantics@int};
\item long note: \res{sum/A-AUDIT/requirements.L2.long_note@int};
\item superseded value: \res{sum/A-AUDIT/requirements.L2.superseded_value@int};

\paragraph{Rule-side items.}
Each of the \res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.n_items@int} items keeps a case fixed and pairs two rules that differ only
in the applicability clause:
\begin{itemize}
\item time window (\res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.by_dimension.window@int});
\item currency (\res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.by_dimension.currency@int});
\item subject (\res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.by_dimension.subject@int});
\item threshold inclusivity, upper and lower (\res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.by_dimension.inclusivity@int}).
\end{itemize}
Each clause has three phrasings. The three cases are the contested form, an uncontested positive, and a
negative: an absent finding, or for threshold items a value clearly on the other side. An item is solved
only if all six judgments are right. A scorer that ignores the rule text, one that never counts the
contested form and one that always counts it all reach crossed accuracy
\res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.validation.crossed_accuracy.ignores_rule_text_counting_rule.XA@int}; the program
reaches \res{sum/A-SETS/xr_v1.validation.crossed_accuracy.program.XA@int}. All rules are synthetic
interventions on grammar scenarios. Items whose contested reading is clinically arguable are listed and
reported separately.
